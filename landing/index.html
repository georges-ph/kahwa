How it works

A page of your own in a couple of minutes, and a link you can put anywhere.

  1. Create your page

    Sign up, add a picture and a few words about what you make, and set the price of a kahwa.

  2. Share your link

    Every creator gets a page with its own address. Post it wherever your audience already follows you.

  3. Get supported

    Fans buy you kahwas and leave a message. Withdraw what you receive through Whish Money.

Made for creators in Lebanon

LBP and USD

Set a price per kahwa in both currencies. Supporters pay in the one they prefer.

Goals

Tell your fans what you are saving for and let them watch the bar fill up.

Private support

Supporters can keep their name and message between them and the creator.

Your color

Pick one of six colors and your whole page takes it.

On your phone, tablet or computer

kahwa runs in the browser and on Android, and its pages rearrange themselves to fit the screen.
